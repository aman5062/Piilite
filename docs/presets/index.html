Presets for DPDP, KYC, GDPR, HIPAA and more

A preset is a ready-made set of PII types for one use case. Pick one with a single option and adjust it with your own options.

js
new Piilite({ preset: "dpdp" });
new Piilite({ preset: "kyc", exclude: ["PINCODE"] }); // your options win

Which presets are available?

PresetUse it forCovers
dpdpIndia's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023Contact details, all Indian IDs, financial, health and employment IDs, device IDs, DOB, vehicle number, GSTIN
kycIdentity verificationAadhaar, VID, PAN, voter ID, passport, DL, ration card, name, address, PIN, DOB, phone, email
fintechPayments, lending, banking supportBank account, UPI, cards, CVV, OTP, PINs, PAN, Aadhaar, GSTIN, IFSC, SWIFT, passwords, contact

healthcareHospitals, telemedicine, insuranceABHA, contact details, Aadhaar, DOB, passport
hrPayroll, hiring, résumésUAN, ESIC, contact details, Aadhaar, PAN, bank, IFSC, passport, DOB, DL
secretsLogs, code, support ticketsAPI keys, private keys, JWTs, tokens, connection strings, passwords, OTPs, PINs
gdprEU personal dataContact details, IDs, financial, health and device data, DOB, IBAN, SWIFT, SSN

strictPrompts sent to third-party modelsEverything, with a lower confidence cut-off (0.4)

What does the DPDP preset cover?

The dpdp preset covers any data that can identify a person under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: names, contact details, Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, passport, bank and UPI details, health IDs, employment IDs, device IDs, date of birth and vehicle numbers.

Presets help you minimise personal data. Compliance also depends on consent, purpose limits, retention and security, so a preset alone does not make an app compliant. This is not legal advice.
